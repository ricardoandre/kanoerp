Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CV Arkananta Garmen Indo ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, application, and related services (the "Service"), including our integrations with TikTok's Marketing API, Content/Login Kit API, and TikTok Shop Partner Center.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information you provide directly, such as your name and email address.
  • Order and transaction data, including purchases made through our store or TikTok Shop.
  • TikTok account information, obtained only after you explicitly authorize our application via TikTok's OAuth process. Depending on the integration you approve, this may include: advertising account and campaign performance data, organic content/video metadata and engagement metrics, and TikTok Shop order, product, and affiliate/creator collaboration data.
  • Usage data, such as pages visited and actions taken on our Service, collected automatically via standard web technologies.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Analyze marketing, content, and sales performance across our own TikTok account(s);
  • Process orders and manage affiliate/creator relationships through TikTok Shop;
  • Communicate with you about your account, orders, or support requests;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. TikTok Data and Permissions

Our application connects to TikTok's APIs solely to access data belonging to accounts we own and operate, or accounts that have explicitly granted authorization through TikTok's official OAuth consent flow. We only request the minimum scopes necessary for the features described above, and access can be revoked at any time by the authorizing account through TikTok's account settings.

4. Data Storage and Security

Information is stored on secured servers under our control. We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access tokens obtained from TikTok are stored securely and used only for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us using the details below.

6. Third-Party Services

Our Service may integrate with third-party platforms, including TikTok, TikTok Shop, and our database/hosting providers. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their handling of information, and we encourage you to review them.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
